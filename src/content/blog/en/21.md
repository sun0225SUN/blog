We are initialized at birth.

Looks and build are the UI. Life is the core business.

Across the whole lifecycle we have to keep learning, pick up skills, add to what we have lived through, and meet more people and more things. Bugs show up. We debug, patch, tune, and sometimes rewrite the whole thing.

And at the same time...

This world has people we like, and work we love. Kindness, a straight spine, gentleness, a sense of humor, the ability to be content, the will to keep going.

Life is both the client and the contractor. Each of us ought to be our own product manager: clear about the next step, breaking through what we already are, shipping the next version of ourselves.

I drifted for a long time. By the time I saw that I had to plan a future for real, I was already twenty-one.

That is all right. I am only twenty-one. What comes next is still wide open.

Time has not given up on me. Go after the future you want.
