> Don't try to add more years to your life. Better add more life to your years. — Blaise Pascal

The last day of 2025 was an ordinary day.

In the morning, a technician from the telecom company came to the little courtyard, Fries on the Hour, and installed broadband.

In the afternoon, Cell and I recorded a podcast. The theme was "Like a flower in the wild, like a fish in water."

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155559122.webp)

That evening Zuozuo said she wanted pizza. We called Feiji and got ready to take a taxi into the old town.

It was New Year's Eve. There were not enough cars. So...

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155611368.webp)

The three of us rode a Lalamove truck into Dali Old Town.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155629428.webp)

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155641738.webp)

Between bites, we started looking back at 2025.

Zuozuo said 2025 was the most social year of her life.

Feiji said: you cannot imagine the meals during my stay in the Middle East. Eating was only a way to fill the stomach.

Food, travel, freedom, money, work, choice, growth, company, feeling, happiness, experience. We talked through a lot of it. The year played back in our heads, scene by scene.

Before we left we stopped at Nuannuan Youth Hostel. On the poster wall in the lobby I noticed this poem.

*Passing the sea, a temperate monsoon on my face.*

*In the hours before I am sleepy, the breath of gulls, and cicadas.*

*The wine here drunk a sailboat.*

*I looked at the boat, and got drunk on a glass of summer.*

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155650880.webp)

Dali is subtropical monsoon, and you rarely hear cicadas even in summer. I still love the last two lines.

*The wine here drunk a sailboat. I looked at the boat, and got drunk on a glass of summer.*

The road ahead is long. A small boat drifts with the wind, and for this moment it has stopped here. I looked at it and started looking at myself. Nothing else. Only thanks for summer, and for the life I was given.

We got back to Yinqiao close to 11 p.m. The plan was to stay up and meet 2026. Dali's magnetic field is too strong. Around 11:55 I was asleep.

When I woke it was seven in the morning. The phone was full of blessings. I scrolled through the feeds and felt what everyone was hoping the new year would be.

2025 into 2026. I had not meant to write anything down, until I saw this in a group chat.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20260101155711397.webp)

Yes. For time itself, New Year's Day is just another ordinary day. So what is the point of crossing the year at all?

Because we like it? Yes. Because we like it.

Time is always relative. Only what you feel right now is real.

Metal, wood, water, fire, earth. Sunlight, air, water, soil, space, and time.

The largest thing I understood last year is this: people and plants are not different in kind.

[Watch on Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1JU4czXEZZ)

Do the thing you like. Grow the way a tree grows, freely.

Be gentle with the years. Give life to the time.

> This short life is something we will lose in the end. You might as well be bold. Love a person. Climb a mountain. Chase a dream. — *Big Fish & Begonia*
