Three days off for New Year's, and Yu and I finally made it to Zhuhai. We had been plotting this one for a while.

## 2023.12.30

We left on time at 8:30 in the morning. About four hours later we were at the hotel.

[The ride on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/10459647745)

After a short rest we headed for a nearby village and lunch.

The courtyard was lovely. It had the feeling of one of those "courtyards with a breeze," even though this was not Dali.

Lunch was chicken stir-fried with ginger and scallion in an iron pot. The verdict was ordinary. I suddenly missed the braised dishes from home, and a dead-dough griddle cake. I could have eaten one into the ground.

After lunch we went back to the hotel. At 5:30 in the evening we left on time, toward the sea.

Around 7 p.m. we reached Lovers' Road. A complaint about this stretch in Xiangzhou: it was rough. Truly rough.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022330642.webp)

From Lovers' Road you can see the Zhuhai Opera House off in the distance.

On the beach at Xianglu Bay we talked for a while with a few fearless kids about the Mazu temple. They had no bikes, and they knew road bikes cold. Then we went to see the Zhuhai Fisher Girl and the Love Post Office.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022335176.webp)

Around 8 p.m. we had hot pot at Three Bulls. It was good enough that the two of us forgot to take a photo. We were busy eating.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022336693.webp)

On the way back we stopped for a bit on the beach at Haitian Park. The view there is ridiculous. It looks like a film. The atmosphere is turned all the way up.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022339536.webp)

## 2023.12.31

At 10 in the morning we had dim sum at Baotang Teahouse, and the Lingnan morning was full of ordinary life.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022341741.webp)

After tea we set off for the New Yuanming Palace. It takes the old Yuanmingyuan in Beijing as its source, and rebuilds eighteen of the forty famous scenes at one-to-one scale.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022346412.webp)

Around 2:40 in the afternoon we reached the platform that looks toward Macau. The fog was too thick. The Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macau Bridge hid inside it.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022349820.webp)

At 3:30 we sat in the Wood-Tone Café and waited for the fog to lift. The room is literary in the way it is furnished, and I got to drink a coffee Yu made himself.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401022355472.webp)

The fog never left. So we decided to ride onto the artificial island and look at the bridge ourselves.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030006060.webp)

At 9:30 that night we had all-you-can-eat barbecue at Hanyangting, then headed to Zhongshan to sleep.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030020702.webp)

We reached the hotel after midnight. Firecrackers and cheering came in through the window.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030022598.webp)

## 2024.01.01

On the first day of the year I had agreed to meet [Simon](https://github.com/Simon-He95) in person, so I needed to get back to Guangzhou a little early.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030025532.webp)

The destination was about 90 kilometers away. Leave at seven and I could be there before noon.

But... if I was going to be up early anyway (in truth I had not slept), why not go see a sunrise?

Zhongshan has two excellent places for it. Lion Head Mountain, and the coast at Yakou. Neither of them was on the way.

They were not on the way. I went anyway. So...

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030029536.webp)
![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030030389.webp)
![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030029107.webp)
![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030031771.webp)

Coming out of Yakou, I checked the time. 8:09. The destination was now exactly 100 kilometers away.

In theory, if I skipped rests and red lights and held an average above 25 km/h, I could still make noon. So...

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030033754.webp)

After a stretch of riding at the limit, I gave in. One section of Highway 228 is genuinely wretched.

In the end I rode as far as Nansha and called a Lalamove truck.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030035192.webp)

At the very end, around 12:30, I did meet Simon. We sat and talked about ordinary things, happy to be there.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030037723.webp)

## Afterword

Three days of the New Year, more than 350 kilometers on the bike. A long ride, and a kind of practice.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030322419.webp)

Thank you to [Yu](https://github.com/ddy-ddy) for planning it with so much care. The trip was full, precious, and hard to forget.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202401030912805.webp)

And thank you to the version of me that will set out anytime, regardless. Feel the wind. Listen to the sea. Touch it. Arrive.
