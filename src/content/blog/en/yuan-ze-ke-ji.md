At the end of 2023 I quit my job in Guangzhou and went to Tibet.

At the start of 2024, the night before my twenty-third birthday, I scrolled past a job post on X.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202403100618031.webp)

https://guoxiaoli.com/post/548.html

The more I typed out the mission and the values of 原则科技, the more it looked like a real chance.

So I sent a résumé. I interviewed in the morning and had an offer that afternoon. Maybe this was the best arrangement the day could make.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202403100623148.webp)

## Hello, Beijing

I sat on a train all night and reached Beijing in the early morning of March 1, 2024.

It was my first time in the city, a place thick with history and with stories people still tell.

A college classmate I am close to is here for graduate school, so we agreed to walk Beijing together on the 2nd.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202403100626309.webp)

We drank soy milk, ate mixed noodles, and wandered around Jingshan Park and the Temple of Heaven.

Beijing's buildings are enormous. They give you a feeling of scale, of something vast.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202403100628315.webp)

## Hello, Yuanze

March 4, a Monday.

I walked 6.6 kilometers and arrived at the office at 9:57 a.m. Onboarding was set for 10:00.

On the first day Miaomiao registered my details. That afternoon I sat with the company knowledge base.

> This society is not short of people who can be used as tools, and it is not short of specialists. What it lacks are people who have a real skill and can still see the whole board. Not knowing is fine. If we don't know, we can learn. If you will not learn and you have no craft, what are you standing on?
>
> I hope you really understand this. Here there is no boss. We are all classmates. We are simply doing the work together. Each of us is responsible to the company and to our own growth. We work in order to become more capable.
>
> If every one of us lived by the constraints of convention and kept ourselves going on inertia, then... the world would be unbearably dull.
>
> — Guo Xiaoli, *We Are Building a Company, Not Merely Working*

No boss. All classmates. That is also why I took the name Xiao Sun. I have graduated, and I am still a student, because you learn until you are old.

原则科技 matches the way I want to live. ProcessOn and Mubu, the products Xiaoli made before this, are both things I like.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202403100637275.webp)

Why is the company called **原则科技**, a name that means principles?

Xiaoli wrote:

> Whether you are being a person, doing a piece of work, or running a company, you should keep a few basic principles. They cut down the mistakes. Learn more, practice more, and you might still be here at the end.
>
> — Guo Xiaoli, *原则科技 and Guo Xiaoli*

Why **start a company again**?

Xiaoli wrote:

> I am an ordinary person. I don't want a job. I chose to start a company, and I know most people don't want a job either.
>
> They don't want to watch the boss's face. They don't want to write a daily report. They don't want meetings that never end. They don't want to spend day after day on work they dislike.
>
> Some of them have started to wake up. They are trying. They are feeding themselves on Douyin, on Bilibili, on Xiaohongshu.
>
> They are brave. They dare to break the usual pattern. They dare to stand on their own.
>
> They fail, and they try again. At the start the income is low. They stay because they are interested.
>
> I want to build tools for them, and help them with what I have. I came up the same road. I tried several times. I was lucky.
>
> — Guo Xiaoli, *Hiring Co-founders, 2024-02-21*

## Fifteen months later

I am sorry I could not stay longer. Half a year after I joined, the company's situation forced me to leave.

This was still where I started building something of my own. I am grateful to Xiaoli, and to 原则科技.
