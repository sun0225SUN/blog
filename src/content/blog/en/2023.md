### Trying to lose weight

The life of 2023 was spent on the road, and on the road of losing weight, and of getting healthier.

Bad habits had me looking swollen by graduation in June.

I did not see how serious it was until the scale passed 170 jin, about 85 kilograms.

I bought a gym card, kept running, watched what I ate, and slept properly. In four months I lost about 20 jin, around 10 kilograms, and my body got healthier.

## A plan for the work

Yes. Half a year after graduation, I quit. Why?

It was not a moment of impulse. It had more to do with the plan I have for the work.

The job I am doing now is web frontend engineering, and I expect that to stay true for a long time. A year ago I did not even understand web development. I was interested, and I had copied and pasted a little code.

### The internship at NIO

I did not look up at the stars, and for a long time I did not see the sunset.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191832280.webp)

I was a genuine novice then. I still am. A bit better than before.

To keep up with the assembly line of a large company, I had to spend more time inside the code.

That spring, outside of study and work, I had almost no time that was mine.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191833887.webp)

The good part is that I truly like the work.

I am grateful for that stretch. It showed me the direction of the work ahead.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191834165.webp)

### I like building for the web

I like to tinker. Development is tinkering. I like this line of work.

During the pandemic, stuck at home and unwilling to sit through online classes, I started taking the computer apart in my head.

Back at school, in the C lab, I saw what that black window could do. I rolled up my sleeves and decided to study it properly.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191835333.webp)

The terminal was too ugly. I wanted a graphical interface, so I started on C#.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191836229.webp)

What I made could not be shared directly. The packaged file still had to be installed, and sometimes it would not run because of the environment. For all of those reasons I lost interest in desktop GUI work completely.

I came to web development because I was building a personal blog. From registering a domain to putting the site online, a web project is simple, and it is ready when you are. I was delighted.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191836603.webp)

### The job at Tuhui

NIO had no headcount, so I had to find another road. By chance I landed at a company in Guangzhou, ready to do something large.

But...

After a while I saw that the way a small company splits the work, and the way it builds its rules, is hard to praise. The thought of leaving showed up early.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/202402191837945.webp)

### Working for myself

At a job we have two bosses.

The first is your superior. You do what is asked, you put in the labor, you take the salary.

The second is yourself. Who you are becoming, what growth you take from the work, which aims you finish: that boss is the one who asks those things of you.

I actually like working. In the work I can grow, which is what people mean by working for yourself.

Quantity turns into quality. Drive yourself, grow, break through, ship the next version. Maybe one day we get free of the first boss, and work only for ourselves. Or stop working for anyone.
