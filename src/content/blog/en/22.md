## Goodbye, campus

The 2023 graduate entrance exam is over. Some people are getting ready for the interview. Others are throwing résumés out in a hurry.

At this stage almost everyone looks a little scared. The future will not sit still, and there is not much we can do about that.

For myself, I decided to leave campus and walk straight at the uncertainty.

## Hello, NIO

I am not from a computer-science program. I am still an undergraduate. I never went through the grind of interview trivia and algorithms, and still I arrived at NIO.

It sounds impossible. Fate brought me here anyway.

After a week at NIO I was sure. This is the future I want.

## Twenty-two. Happy birthday.

Shape the character. Let the skill settle. Make something that matters.
