I was lucky. At twenty I found the thing I can love for the rest of my life: coding.

Zeros and ones, put in the right order, are what push an era forward. There is enough energy in code to change the world.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20250830210507336.webp)

---

The road in was not a smooth one.

Along it I met teachers who handed over what they knew, and friends who wanted the same things I did. I felt the pull of this world of programs, and the good temper of the communities around it.

I got somewhere, in a small way. I started writing a blog, and I put up a site of my own at https://guoqi.dev.

![](https://files.guoqi.dev/images/20250830210820989.webp)

On the technical side, I can now build simple web projects on my own.
